Behind the Porch

Made for the music. Inspired by the good times.

Panic on the Porch was born from a love of Widespread Panic, Southern music, and everything that comes with it. From unforgettable nights at shows to the stories told long after the music stops, we're here to celebrate the culture that brings us all together.

Our merchandise is inspired by the music, the memories, and the people who make every show feel like coming home. Whether you're on the road chasing the next show or kicking back on the porch, we've got something for you.
